CENTRALISED MASTER PAGES - RAJKOT AIRPORT MEDIA GAMMA DECKS

Use this master prompt structure for every individual AD board, package, or media-kit deck.

The goal is to keep all decks consistent:
- Slide 1 is always the same airport media intro structure.
- Slide 2 puts inventory and commercial data upfront for transparency.
- Slide 3 explains why Rajkot Airport is a strong airport media buy.
- Middle slides show the asset/package proof using current source content and updated real images.
- One slide introduces the operator/team credibility.
- Contact / next-step comes before the final disclaimer.
- The final slide is always terms and disclaimer.

Only images should be manually replaced from the current PPT/PDF.

MASTER MIDDLE SLIDES - ASSET-SPECIFIC CONTENT

Start from Slide 2.

For every asset/package, follow this flow:

Slide 2 - Transparent inventory and commercial snapshot:
Show the main facts upfront so buyers feel the offer is direct and organized:
- Asset code or package name
- Format
- Size or unit count
- Exact location / zone
- Rental charge
- GST note
- Availability subject to confirmation
- Any creative or slot conditions that apply

Slide 3 - Why Rajkot Airport:
Position Rajkot Airport as a premium, practical media buy without saying "best" as an unsupported claim.
Use this headline direction:
"Why Rajkot Airport is a strong media buy for premium regional reach."

Use the confirmed Ridham facts:
- Approx. 1.25-1.30 lakh passenger visits per month
- Approx. 4,200-4,400 passenger visits per day
- 14 arrivals + 14 departures per day
- 28 total flight movements per day

Buyer logic to include:
- Captive airport movement, not generic roadside clutter
- Premium context for regional, NRI, business, and family-travel audiences
- Useful for categories that need trust, recall, and visible presence
- Operator-confirmed figures should be verified before final release

Slide 4 - Asset/package location and visual proof:
Paraphrase the current PPT/PDF content into buyer-friendly language. Keep the original location truth. Explain why the placement matters without adding unverified passenger numbers.

Slide 5 - Team and operating support:
Show why the buyer can trust execution:
- Operated by Mukesh Arts
- Owner-led response and coordination
- Ridham Bhuva's ASCO-certified operations support smoother site visits, airport protocol coordination, and creative approval handling
- Site visits can be coordinated before final booking discussion
- Creative approval and airport protocol coordination handled by the operator team

Slide 6 - Contact / next step:
Make the ask simple:
- Share campaign month
- Share preferred asset/package
- Share creative format
- Share booking duration
- Operator team confirms availability, commercial terms, site visit, production needs, and approval steps

Slide 7 - Terms and disclaimer:
Protect the operator and avoid wrong conduct. This is always the final slide.

Optional slide - Specifications and commercial details:
Use a structured table. Include only confirmed details from the deck prompt.

Optional slide - Best-fit campaigns:
Use only generic category-fit recommendations. No fake proof, no invented clients, no guaranteed performance.

Optional creative flexibility note:
For applicable digital inventory only, say: "Flexible creative scheduling, multiple creatives within one booked slot, and live/scheduled updates can be discussed subject to operator scheduling and approval." Do not apply this wording to static boards unless a creative replacement process is separately confirmed.

Each middle slide must include an IMAGE BLOCK so the real image can be manually inserted.

MASTER FINAL SLIDE - TERMS AND DISCLAIMER

Use this as the final slide for every deck.

Headline:
Terms and media conditions

Body:
- All inventory is subject to availability at the time of written confirmation.
- Rental charges are quoted for the stated campaign period and are exclusive of GST unless specified otherwise.
- Creative, artwork, printing, mounting, and production costs, if applicable, are separate from media rental unless specifically included in writing.
- All creatives are subject to airport/operator approval before display.
- Passenger visit and flight movement figures are operator-confirmed estimates and should be verified before final campaign release.
- Flexible creative updates, multiple creatives in one slot, and live/scheduled updates apply only to eligible inventory and are subject to operator scheduling, technical feasibility, and approval.
- Political, religious, racial, misleading, or prohibited creative content may be rejected.
- Final booking is confirmed only after written approval, payment confirmation, and completion of required campaign formalities.
- Locations, formats, and display schedules should be verified before campaign release.

Layout:
Off White background with a clean two-column terms layout. Use a small Saffron "Important" label. Keep this professional and protective, not scary.
