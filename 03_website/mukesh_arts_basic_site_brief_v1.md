# Mukesh Arts Basic Website Brief v1

Date: 2026-05-24

## Purpose

The Mukesh Arts website should be a simple credibility site, not a full sales engine.

Its job is to answer:

- Who is Mukesh Arts?
- What kind of advertising work does the company do?
- Why is it credible locally?
- How can someone contact the team?

The airport media website remains the main commercial priority.

## Recommended Format

One-page static website.

A three-page version is acceptable only if needed:

- Home
- Services
- Contact

No blog, no CRM workflow, no rate-card system, no complex content engine.

## Page Sections

### 1. Hero

Headline:

"Mukesh Arts"

Support line:

"Local outdoor and airport media operator based in Gujarat."

CTA:

"Contact Us"

### 2. About

Short company profile:

- Local advertising/operator background
- Relationship-led business style
- Practical execution capability
- Current airport media work as a credibility signal

Keep this humble and factual.

### 3. Services

Basic services list:

- Outdoor advertising
- Airport media operations
- Static and digital display coordination
- Campaign execution support
- Local vendor coordination

### 4. Rajkot Airport Media

One section only.

Message:

"Mukesh Arts operates airport media inventory at Rajkot International Airport. For inventory, pricing, and campaign inquiries, visit/contact the dedicated airport media page."

This should send serious buyers to the airport site.

### 5. Contact

Include:

- Phone/WhatsApp
- Email
- City/region
- Simple contact form if easy

## Visual Direction

Keep it clean and restrained:

- Light background
- Simple typography
- Few real photos
- No heavy animation
- No over-polished corporate claims

This site should feel established and trustworthy, not like a startup pitch deck.

## Build Priority

Build this only after the airport site and media kit are pitch-ready.
