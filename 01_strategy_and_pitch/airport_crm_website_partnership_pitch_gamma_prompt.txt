CARD 11 - SUCCESS FEE MODEL

Headline:
Low fixed cost, upside only when the system helps book business.

Success-fee principle:
The monthly fee is kept founder-friendly. If the website/CRM system helps create a new booked client, a small success fee applies only to that CRM-originated deal.

Recommended terms:
3-4% of the total confirmed media booking value for that CRM-originated campaign
Preferred success fee: 4%
Friendly negotiation floor: 3%
Applies only to new leads captured or clearly tracked through the website/CRM system before closure
GST, printing, production, mounting, fabrication, and vendor charges excluded from fee calculation
Payable only after client payment is received
Existing Mukesh Arts / owner relationship deals are excluded unless explicitly entered and managed as new CRM-originated opportunities
For example: if a CRM-originated client books INR 3,00,000/month for 6 months, total media booking value is INR 18,00,000. A 4% success fee equals INR 72,000; a 3.5% success fee equals INR 63,000; a 3% success fee equals INR 54,000.
Optional repeat fee: 2% on repeat booking from the same CRM-originated client within 90 days

Why this is fair:
Mukesh Arts keeps control of pricing and closure. The digital system earns upside only when it creates trackable business.

Visual direction:
Use a clean commercial model graphic: low retainer + CRM-originated upside. Avoid aggressive commission language.

Layout:
Two columns: "Fixed partnership fee" and "Performance upside".
