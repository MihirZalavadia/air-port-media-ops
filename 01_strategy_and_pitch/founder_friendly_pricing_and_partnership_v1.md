# Founder-Friendly Partnership Pricing v1

Date: 2026-06-03

## Positioning

Mukesh Arts already has the hard part: OOH experience, local relationships, airport media inventory, and owner-led execution. The proposed partnership builds the missing digital sales layer around that strength:

- Airport marketing website
- CRM lead pipeline
- Owner dashboard
- Follow-up tracker
- Proposal and media-kit system
- Optional Mukesh Arts credibility website

The pricing should feel founder-friendly and relationship-first, but not undervalue the work. The minimum acceptable monthly price is INR 28,000.

The pitch should be practical, not overhyped: this is not "software for software's sake." It is a simple control room for their next stage of airport media growth, so leads, follow-ups, quotes, and booked revenue do not stay scattered across calls, WhatsApp, memory, and separate files.

## Success Fee

Use this framing:

"We keep the fixed price founder-friendly. If our website/CRM system helps create a new booked client, then we earn a small success fee only on that CRM-originated campaign."

Recommended terms:

- 3-4% of the total confirmed media booking value for that CRM-originated campaign
- Preferred success fee: 4%
- Friendly negotiation floor: 3%
- Applies only to new leads captured or clearly tracked through the website/CRM system before closure
- Excludes GST, production, printing, mounting, fabrication, and vendor costs
- Payable only after the client payment is received
- Existing Mukesh Arts/Ridham/Mukesh sir relationship deals are excluded unless they are explicitly entered and managed as new CRM-originated opportunities
- Optional repeat fee: 2% on repeat booking from the same CRM-originated client within 90 days

Example:

If a CRM-originated client books INR 3,00,000/month for 6 months, the total media booking value is INR 18,00,000.

- At 4%, success fee = INR 72,000
- At 3.5%, success fee = INR 63,000
- At 3%, success fee = INR 54,000

Avoid this wording:

- "Commission on all deals"
- "Cut from your business"
- "We own the client"

Use this wording:

- "CRM-originated lead success fee"
- "Performance upside only when the system helps book business"
- "Existing relationships stay yours"
- "Calculated on confirmed campaign media value, excluding GST and production/vendor costs"

## CRM Automation Explained Simply

Main manual pain points today:

- Leads can come through calls, WhatsApp, referrals, website links, site visits, and proposal forwards.
- Package interest, campaign dates, and budget can get scattered across chats and memory.
- Follow-ups can be missed because there is no shared next-action list.
- Owners may not have one simple view of hot leads, quotes shared, pending calls, and booked deals.
- Lost leads do not always have a reason captured, so the sales process does not improve.

What we manage end to end:

- Website/form inquiries entering the CRM.
- Simple manual entry for WhatsApp/referral leads.
- Dropdown statuses like New, Contacted, Hot, Quote Shared, Site Visit, Booked, and Lost.
- Owner assignment for Mukesh sir, Ridham, or both.
- Follow-up date tracking and daily/weekly action views.
- Package interest and campaign window tracking.
- Quotation/proposal link tracking.
- Booked value, campaign duration, and CRM-originated lead attribution.
- Weekly pipeline summary: new leads, hot leads, stuck leads, follow-ups due, quotes pending, booked value.

Simple meeting line:

"We are not trying to replace your relationship style. We are removing manual leakage. If airport media starts growing, every inquiry, quote, follow-up, and booked campaign needs to be visible in one place."

## Negotiation Script

Start with:

"The full 4-month system is INR 45,000 per month. Because this is relationship-first and we want to build trust, I can keep it founder-friendly. But I do not want to do only design work. The value is the website plus CRM plus lead follow-up system."

If they hesitate:

"We can start with a 1-month airport website sprint at INR 38,000. If you like the output, we move into CRM and dashboard from month two."

If they push price hard:

"I can come down, but not below INR 28,000 per month because the work includes strategy, website, CRM setup, proposal system, and ongoing pipeline support. Below that it becomes only basic design work, and that will not solve the business problem."

For success fee:

"I will not ask for anything from your existing relationship deals. But if a new client comes through the website/CRM system and books, then a 3-4% success fee on the confirmed campaign media value is fair because the system helped create and track that business. For example, INR 3,00,000 per month for 6 months is INR 18,00,000 total media value; 4% is INR 72,000."
