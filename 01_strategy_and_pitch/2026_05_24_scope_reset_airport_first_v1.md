# Scope Reset - Airport First, Mukesh Arts Basic

Date: 2026-05-24

## Decision

The airport media operation is the main project.

We should not treat the airport website and the Mukesh Arts website as equal builds. The airport project gets the ambitious sales infrastructure: website, media kit, inventory visibility, CRM, outreach, and pitch-grade positioning.

Mukesh Arts gets a basic credibility website only. It should support trust, legal/operator identity, and local business presence, but it should not become a second complex brand system right now.

## Why

- The 90-day plan is measured on airport inventory sales, qualified leads, active conversations, and pipeline value.
- The Year 1 airport concession window is time-sensitive. Blank screens and unsold inventory are the revenue leak.
- Buyers need to understand the airport offer quickly: inventory, audience, pricing, compliance, and contact path.
- Mukesh Arts is important as operator credibility, but a full personal/company website will not close airport media deals by itself.
- One strong sales property is better than two half-built websites.

## Brand Hierarchy

Use this hierarchy until final legal naming is confirmed:

1. Primary commercial offer: Rajkot Airport Media / Rajkot Airport Advertising (working name TBC)
2. Operator credibility: Operated by Mukesh Arts
3. Trust layer: Rajkot International Airport, AAI partnership language, ASCO-certified operations
4. People layer: Mukesh sir and Ridham appear in About/Contact, not as the main hero message

Practical rule: the airport site should lead with the airport media opportunity, not with Mukesh Arts as the headline brand.

## Airport Website Scope

The airport website is the main B2B sales asset.

It should include:

- Strong airport-first hero
- Inventory and package overview
- Real pricing where approved
- Real on-site visuals from the media kit assets
- AAI/compliance trust signals, without overclaiming
- Audience and corridor story for Saurashtra/NRI/HNI segments
- Lead capture form wired later to CRM
- Download/request media kit CTA
- Contact path for Ridham/Mukesh sir

It should avoid:

- Generic agency language
- Stock airport photos where real site photos exist
- Big unverified passenger numbers
- Making Mukesh Arts the first-viewport brand unless legally required
- A founder-story-heavy structure

## Mukesh Arts Website Scope

Mukesh Arts gets a simple web presence.

It should include:

- Home/about section
- Services overview
- Local OOH/advertising experience
- Rajkot Airport media concession as one credibility section
- Contact details
- Optional gallery

It should avoid:

- CRM-heavy workflows
- Full media kit or rate tables
- Tender intelligence
- Blog/content engine
- Multi-page product architecture
- Any positioning that competes with the airport site

## This Week's Priority

1. Use the airport-first brief as the source of truth for website work.
2. Build/refine the airport site before spending more time on Mukesh Arts.
3. Keep Mukesh Arts as a basic, clean, later-stage site.
4. Update decks and prompts so "Mukesh Arts" reads as operator credibility, not the main commercial product.
