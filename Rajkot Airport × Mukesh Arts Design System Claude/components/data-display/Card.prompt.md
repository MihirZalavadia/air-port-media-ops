**Card** — the surface primitive. Square corners, hairline border, soft hover lift with reddening border.

```jsx
<Card>
  <Eyebrow>01</Eyebrow>
  <h3>Premium context</h3>
  <p>Airport movement gives brands a cleaner environment…</p>
</Card>
<Card interactive={false} padding={24}>Static panel</Card>
```

Compose headings (Cormorant), Eyebrow, body, and dividers inside. Don't round the corners.
